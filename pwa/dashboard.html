Zone · Phase 1

See it coming — while the network is still up.

This page is the online half of Zone: a situational-awareness view for a control room or an NGO desk that still has a connection. It watches the planet's open hazard data — earthquakes, fires, disaster alerts, public traffic cameras — so you can see what is developing, and where, before anything breaks.

Phase 1 · this page

Everything here needs internet. It is recorded and read while the world is connected — the "before" picture.

Phase 2 · Zone, the main product

When the towers go down, the Zone app takes over: phones relay tiny 31-byte status signals over Bluetooth, and the Dead Man's Packet turns a silent phone into an early warning. No network. No servers.

About the cameras. The feeds shown are official open-data traffic cameras (London and Hong Kong). They show what a public feed looks like — they are not cameras inside a disaster zone, which would lose power and connectivity in the same event that takes down the network. That is exactly the gap Zone's mesh is built for.
