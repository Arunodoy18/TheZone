This page is the online half of Zone — it is not the phone app. The Citizen/Responder/Map app on the phone needs no network, ever, and keeps working exactly the same whether or not this page exists. This dashboard is for a control room, an NGO desk, or the edge of the blackout zone — wherever a connection already exists — to see Zone's own mesh picture laid over open, free hazard data.

Reading this map
Zone markers are the mesh's own picture, carried out of the blackout by a responder or a phone that regained signal — not live-tracked positions. A dark ring marks a cell that went fully silent. Everything else on this page is public open data, shown only for context; it is never sent into the mesh or the phone app.
