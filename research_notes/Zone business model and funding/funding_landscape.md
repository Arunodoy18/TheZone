# Funding Landscape for Pre-DPIIT, Pre-Incorporation Student Team (SMIT, Sikkim) — "Zone"

## What student-level/institution-level innovation grants exist that don't require incorporation/DPIIT?

### Takeaway
NIDHI-PRAYAS (DST) is the most promising no-incorporation grant mechanism (up to ₹10 lakh, named eligibility for "students, researchers and innovators"), but several sources flag a strict requirement that the funded project involve **physical product development**, explicitly excluding pure software — this is a real risk for an Android-app project and needs direct confirmation with a PRAYAS Centre before relying on it. AICTE has an Innovation & Entrepreneurship ecosystem (IDEA Labs, Student Startup Policy) that is institutional/academic support rather than direct cash grants for a team at this stage; no live 2026 AICTE cash-grant program aimed at undergraduate student founders was found.

### Cited Findings
- NIDHI-PRAYAS: an aggregator describes it as funding up to ₹10 lakh to turn a concept into a working prototype, with eligibility explicitly naming "students, researchers and innovators," and states "no incorporation required." — [fundsforngos.org via search synthesis](https://www2.fundsforngos.org/information-technology/nidhi-prayas-program-in-india/)
- IIT Kanpur's NIDHI-PRAYAS guidelines state: "The project should involve physical product development. The projects relating to software development and those involving pure academic research are not eligible." — [IIT Kanpur NIDHI-PRAYAS Guidelines](https://iitk.ac.in/invent/NIDHI-PRAYAS-Guidelines.html)
- A-Hub (Ahmedabad University) page distinguishes "Individual Innovators" (Indian citizen, 18+, no incorporation needed) from "Startups" (which must be incorporated in India) as separate eligible applicant categories under NIDHI-PRAYAS. — [Ahmedabad University A-Hub](https://ahduni.edu.in/academics/schools-centres/venturestudio/programmes/funding-programmes/nidhi-prayas)
- Grant mechanics: up to ₹10 lakh disbursed, project term up to 18 months (extendable 6 months), administered through a designated "PRAYAS Centre" at a host incubator (e.g., iCreate). — [FundsforNGOs](https://www2.fundsforngos.org/latest-funds-for-ngos/nidhi-prayas-program-india-for-innovators/)
- IIITDM Kancheepuram's page on NIDHI-PRAYAS notes IP ownership and an NOC from the parent institution are required where applicable, and that the grant cannot fund a student internship at an academic/R&D institution. — [IIITDM Kancheepuram PRAYAS page](https://www.madeit.iiitdm.ac.in/home/prayas)
- AICTE's Student Startup Policy (2016) is institutional support (academic flexibility for students running registered startups), not a direct cash grant program. — [search synthesis of AICTE material](https://aicte.gov.in/node/290)
- AICTE's newest 2026 funding item found, the Innovation & Entrepreneurship Doctoral Fellowship Programme (AIEDFP), launched Sept 3, 2026 with ₹245 crore outlay and an Innovation Grant up to ₹10 lakh — but this targets **PhD researchers**, not undergraduate student founders. — [GKToday](https://www.gktoday.in/aicte-launches-%e2%82%b9245-crore-doctoral-fellowship-programme/)
- An aggregator (startupgrantsindia.com) claims an AICTE/state "SSIP 2.0" with a ₹500 crore corpus and per-team seed grants of ₹2.5–10 lakh, but this could not be confirmed against any official AICTE or government source. — [StartupGrantsIndia](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/providers/aicte) (unconfirmed, treat with caution)

### Inferences
- Zone is a software-only Android app with no physical hardware component, which likely makes it ineligible for NIDHI-PRAYAS under the "physical product development" rule as described by IIT Kanpur — this should be verified directly with whichever PRAYAS Centre the team would apply through (some centres may interpret "product" more broadly to include a deployable system with hardware-adjacent elements like BLE radios, but this is not confirmed).
- The team's most realistic near-term path for a cash grant without incorporation is a hackathon prize (see SIH below) or an institutional seed grant through SMIT's own IIC/pre-incubation cohort, not a DST/AICTE mechanism.

### Gaps
- No confirmed, current (2026) NIDHI-PRAYAS call or open PRAYAS Centre reachable from Sikkim/SMIT was identified — the team should check nidhi.dst.gov.in directly and contact a nearby PRAYAS Centre (e.g., via IIM Calcutta Innovation Park, which is active in the Northeast — see below) to ask explicitly whether a BLE-hardware-adjacent software app for disaster communication qualifies as "physical product development."
- No AICTE direct-to-student-team cash grant program (distinct from the PhD fellowship and from SIH) was confirmed as currently open in 2026.

## Is SIH 2026/2027 open, what do winners get, does it require DPIIT, and what's the cycle?

### Takeaway
SIH is run by AICTE/Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell, is entered through a college SPOC with no DPIIT/incorporation requirement, and the 2026 cycle has effectively already moved past its September college-internal and idea-submission stages toward a December grand finale — meaning SIH 2026 is likely too late for Zone to enter now, but SIH 2027 (expected to launch mid-2027) is a clean, no-incorporation-needed opportunity to flag for later.

### Cited Findings
- SIH 2026 is organized by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell and AICTE; entry is through each college's registered SPOC (Single Point of Contact) on sih.gov.in, not by individual/direct application. — [search synthesis, multiple college pages](https://sih.gov.in)
- Team size is fixed at exactly 6 students including the team leader, with at least one female member required on each team. — [search synthesis of SIH 2026 college circulars]
- Timeline (third-party-reported, not confirmed on sih.gov.in directly): launch ~August 21, 2026; internal college hackathons and idea-PPT submission in September–November 2026; 36-hour non-stop Grand Finale at nodal centers nationwide in December 2026. — [Reskilll blog](https://blogs.reskilll.com/smart-india-hackathon-2026-launched-timeline-registration-how-to-participate/)
- One college (PSCMR) lists a prize of ₹1,50,000 per winning team/problem statement, "awarded by the collaborating ministry/industry subject to its acceptance of the winning solution" — this is a college-reported figure, not confirmed on the official portal, and other past SIH cycles are commonly reported (in general knowledge, not verified here) to include post-win incubation support from AICTE/MeitY-linked incubators. — [PSCMR listing via Internshala](https://internshala.com/competitions/?p=9657)
- No DPIIT or incorporation requirement was found anywhere in SIH eligibility material; it is explicitly a student/college competition entered via academic SPOC. — [search synthesis]
- I could not directly reach sih.gov.in in this research session; all dates/prizes above come from third-party blogs and college circulars, some of which conflict (one aggregator says "exact dates not specified by the organizer"). — [Internshala](https://internshala.com/competitions/smart-india-hackathon-sih-2026/)

### Inferences
- Because SIH 2026's internal/college-level deadlines (mid-to-late September 2026) have already passed as of today (Oct 23, 2026), and the Grand Finale is reportedly set for December 2026, Zone's team cannot practically enter the current SIH cycle through the normal SPOC pathway unless their specific SMIT SPOC has a later internal timeline than the colleges sampled here.
- SIH typically runs annually; a 2027 cycle (likely launching mid-to-late 2027) should be treated as the next viable entry point, and the team should ask SMIT's SIH SPOC directly about whether any late-entry or institute-level wildcard route exists for the current cycle.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm the official prize structure, post-win incubation terms, or exact current-phase status directly from sih.gov.in — the search tool did not surface the official portal page itself. The team should check sih.gov.in and ask their SMIT SIH SPOC directly about current-cycle status.
- No information found on whether SIH winners get any guaranteed follow-on funding/incubation seat beyond the cash prize claimed by one college.

## Northeast-India-specific schemes for pre-incorporation teams (NEC, DoNER, Startup Northeast)

### Takeaway
The North East Entrepreneurship Development Programme (NEEDP) — a North Eastern Council (NEC) and IIM Calcutta Innovation Park (IIMCIP) collaboration with a ₹7.5 crore corpus targeting 270 start-ups across the 8 NE states, explicitly designed to help **student entrepreneurs** — is the single most relevant Northeast-specific lead found, and IIMCIP is also now Sikkim's own Lead Incubation Partner, which may give the SMIT team a direct, local on-ramp. No DPIIT/incorporation requirement was confirmed or denied for NEEDP in the sources found — this needs direct follow-up.

### Cited Findings
- NEEDP: launched September 2022, a collaboration between IIM Calcutta Innovation Park and the North Eastern Council; targets finding and funding 270 start-ups from the 8 NE states with a total budget of ₹7.5 crore; "specially designed to help student entrepreneurs," offering training, mentoring, and startup money. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/iim-calcutta-innovation-park-north-east-entrepreneurship-start-up-assam-meghalaya-arunachal-pradesh)
- Sikkim's Commerce & Industries Department has signed a Memorandum of Agreement with IIM Calcutta Innovation Park, appointing IIMCIP as Lead Incubation Partner via the "Sikkim Innovation Hub," a 36-month partnership supporting the Sikkim Startup Policy 2025 under the "Sikkim INSPIRES" programme, with planned district-level incubation centres (including at ATTC, Bardang and CCCT, Chisopani). — [Sikkim Industries Dept circular](https://industries.sikkim.gov.in/circulardoc/circulardoc-HBrVZ9CRo66PlXKpcvgoOqaHHTMcECe6NFsvsQHq.pdf); [EntrepreneurIndia](https://www.entrepreneurindia.com/blog/en/news/sikkim-government-partners-with-iimcip-to-support-250-startups.61210)
- The separate, older NEC "IIE entrepreneurship development and incubation centres in NE colleges" item is undated/unsourced beyond a passing news reference and could not be confirmed as currently active. — [search synthesis, no reliable primary source found]
- NEC also runs a "Financial Support to Students of NER for Higher Professional Courses" (NEC Merit Scholarship) — but this is an education/tuition scholarship (₹20,000–25,000/year via the National Scholarship Portal, income-capped at ₹8 lakh/year parental income), not a startup or innovation grant, and should not be confused with NEEDP. — [amkresourceinfo.com](https://amkresourceinfo.com/financial-support-to-the-students-of-ner-for-higher-professional-courses-2025-26-applications-open/)

### Inferences
- Given IIMCIP's dual role (NEEDP administrator for the whole Northeast, and now Sikkim's own state-appointed Lead Incubation Partner), the single highest-value, lowest-effort next step for the Zone team is almost certainly to contact IIMCIP (or the new Sikkim Innovation Hub) directly and ask whether NEEDP or any Sikkim INSPIRES component can fund a pre-incorporation student team — this channel appears to be purpose-built for exactly this team's profile.
- DoNER ministry itself was not found to run a distinct, separate pre-incorporation grant program beyond funding NEC's initiatives; DoNER appears to act as a funder/policy body rather than a direct-to-team grantor.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm NEEDP's current (2026) application status, whether it is still accepting new cohorts, or its DPIIT/incorporation requirements — direct inquiry to IIM Calcutta Innovation Park or the Sikkim Innovation Hub is needed.
- No DoNER-specific (ministry-direct, not via NEC) student/pre-incorporation scheme was found in this research pass.

## NOT YET ACCESSIBLE — schemes requiring DPIIT recognition and/or incorporation (confirmed, for later reference)

### Takeaway
SISFS (Startup India Seed Fund Scheme) and AIC-SMUTBI's DPIIT-linked seed fund both confirmed to require DPIIT recognition plus incorporation (and, for SISFS, incorporation no more than 2 years before application). SISFS's officially-stated operational window was originally April 2021 to 2025, and no 2026 extension could be confirmed in this research — its current live/closed status should be checked directly at the official portal before the team spends time planning around it.

### Cited Findings
- SISFS core eligibility: DPIIT recognition required; startup incorporated not more than 2 years before applying; Indian promoters must hold ≥51% equity at time of application; technology must be core to the product/service/business model; startup must not have received more than ₹10 lakh from any other central/state government scheme; eligible entity types are Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, or LLP (not an unregistered team). — [multiple consistent sources, e.g. BankBazaar](https://www.bankbazaar.com/government-schemes/startup-india-seed-fund-scheme.html); [FITT-IITD](https://fitt-iitd.in/web/sisfs)
- SISFS funds flow through DPIIT-recognised incubators, not directly to applicants; grant amounts: up to ₹20 lakh (proof of concept/prototype, milestone-based) and up to ₹50 lakh (commercialization, via convertible debentures/debt instruments). — [consistent across sources]
- SISFS's officially cited operational period in multiple (older) sources is "April 1, 2021 to 2025" — a 945 crore corpus. A 2026-dated Bajaj Finserv explainer (April 2026) describes the scheme as apparently still active/describable but does not explicitly confirm an extension beyond 2025. No official DPIIT notice of extension was found in this research. — [Bajaj Finserv, April 2026](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/startup-india-seed-fund-scheme-details); caveat flagged, not confirmed either way
- AIC-SMUTBI's own incubation program (separate from its DPIIT-linked seed fund) does allow unregistered applicants to apply for **incubation** — "Startups with no legal registration may be considered for incubation if the application is submitted in the names of all promoters/founders," but **requires the promoters to register the startup under an eligible entity type within one year of the incubation start date.** This is a meaningful nuance: AIC-SMUTBI incubation itself may not require Day-1 incorporation, but its DPIIT-linked **seed fund** specifically requires DPIIT recognition and incorporation no more than 2 years prior, consistent with SISFS terms generally. — [AIC-SMUTBI official Apply page](https://www.smutbi.com/apply/); [EastMojo, Jan 2022, on DPIIT-linked seed fund](https://www.eastmojo.com/business/finance/2022/01/07/aic-smutbi-selected-by-startup-india-to-provide-financial-assistance-to-startups/)
- AIC-SMUTBI post-registration eligibility: startup must not be older than 10 years from registration/incorporation (15 years for biotech), annual turnover in any preceding year must not exceed ₹25 crore, and must not be formed by splitting/reconstructing an existing business. — [AIC-SMUTBI Apply page](https://www.smutbi.com/apply/)
- DPIIT recognition process/timeline: core required documents are Certificate of Incorporation, business PAN, applicant Aadhaar, authorisation letter, active mobile/email, plus constitutive documents (MoA/AoA for a company, or partnership deed for LLPs/firms). Timeline estimates vary across sources: 2–10 days (PhonePe, 2026 guide), 2–7 working days if documents are in order (Khurana & Khurana), 3–7 days (Setindiabiz, a paid filing service), up to 20–25 business days (Professional Utilities, a paid filing service) — the 2–7 working day range is most consistently cited for a clean, complete application; longer estimates appear to reflect queries/resubmissions. — [PhonePe Business, 2026](https://business.phonepe.com/articles/dpiit-startup-registration-complete-process-benefits-and-documents-2026); [Khurana & Khurana, Jan 2025](https://www.khuranaandkhurana.com/2025/01/29/dpiit-startup-registration)

### Inferences
- The real sequencing gate for Zone is: incorporate (Pvt Ltd or LLP) → apply for DPIIT recognition (likely 1–4 weeks all-in for recognition once incorporated) → only then become eligible for SISFS and AIC-SMUTBI's seed fund (and other DPIIT-gated schemes generally, e.g. income tax exemptions under Section 80-IAC, not researched in depth here but commonly DPIIT-linked).
- AIC-SMUTBI's incubation program itself may be usable *before* incorporation (per its own stated one-year grace period for promoters to register), which could make it a realistic near-term step even pre-DPIIT — this is worth the team confirming directly with AIC-SMUTBI, since it's a meaningfully different (more permissive) rule than SISFS's.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm from an official DPIIT/Startup India source whether SISFS is currently open for new applications as of October 2026, or whether it has lapsed/been renewed since its originally stated 2021–2025 window. The team should check seedfund.startupindia.gov.in directly before assuming it's accessible even once they incorporate.

## Company incorporation (Pvt Ltd or LLP) — real cost and time for a student team

### Takeaway
Based on third-party (mostly commercial filing-service) sources, a 2-director Pvt Ltd company in India costs roughly ₹7,000–₹25,000 all-in and takes roughly 5–15 working days once paperwork (DSC, DIN, PAN, name approval, MoA/AoA) is ready — this is the real-world size of the gap between where the team is now and DPIIT eligibility. No official MCA fee-schedule source was directly reached in this pass; figures should be treated as estimates pending confirmation.

### Cited Findings
- All-in cost estimate: roughly ₹7,000 to ₹25,000 for a two-director Pvt Ltd company. SPICe+ filing fee is ₹0 up to ₹15 lakh authorised capital; DIN, PAN, TAN are free; main variable costs are state stamp duty (higher in states like Maharashtra and Karnataka) and Digital Signature Certificates (~₹800–2,500 per director), plus any professional/filing-agent fees. — [search synthesis of multiple 2026-dated filing-service blogs, e.g. Kanakkupillai, TaxAj, TechStory] (commercial sources; treat as estimates, not official)
- Timeline estimates: "7 to 15 working days once documents are ready" (one source); "5–10 working days if paperwork is clean" (another); a paid-service claim of "done in 7 days" (likely optimistic/promotional).
- Minimum 2 directors required, at least one must be an Indian resident (present in India 182+ days in the previous year) — relevant if any team member studies or travels abroad.
- No minimum paid-up capital requirement found; ₹1 lakh authorised capital is commonly used as a default.
- Post-incorporation obligations: commencement-of-business filing (INC-20A) within 180 days of incorporation; auditor appointment (Form ADT-1) within 30 days — missing these can lead to penalties or company strike-off.

### Inferences
- For a 4-student team, incorporation cost is genuinely low (likely under ₹25,000 total, possibly cheaper using DIY MCA filing vs. a paid agent) and the time (roughly 1–3 weeks) is short relative to a hackathon or grant cycle — the real barrier is less "cost/time" and more "readiness to commit to a formal legal entity and ongoing compliance (annual filings, auditor, ROC returns) while still students," which is a strategic/timing decision rather than a financial one.

### Gaps
- No official Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) fee schedule was directly consulted in this research pass — the team should verify current SPICe+ fees and state-specific stamp duty (for Sikkim specifically) at mca.gov.in before budgeting.
- No Sikkim-specific stamp duty figure was found; this is likely the single largest cost variable and is state-dependent.
